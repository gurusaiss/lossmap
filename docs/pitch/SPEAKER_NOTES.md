# Speaker notes

Say "simulated" every time a demo number appears. Never state an AI accuracy figure; there is none. Never say "live" until the smoke test has passed against the real project. Do not claim a time per checkpoint (not measured) or "agentic".

## Slide by slide
**1.** "LossMap finds where your harvest loses value." Jury: name the track and problem number. Investors: skip the track detail.
**2.** "Produce loses value before it reaches the factory, mostly in the first mile: the national study puts tomato loss at 11.62%, 8.37% at the farm stage. But the record shows only the final weight." Say 100 → 82 kg is an illustration. Jury: answer the brief's own question. Investors: the buyer carries this loss today.
**3.** "Staff already weigh at the centre and factory. We add one scan and two photos; the system reads the scale, adds weather and waiting time, and the manager sees where and why, in rupees." Jury: zero new hardware or staff. Investors: low adoption friction.
**4.** "This is the working MVP on a simulated Madanapalle tomato processor. Centre B loses 11.3% of value, Centre A 4.5%; waiting explains most of it." Demo, then "Scan the QR" (only once a URL exists). Jury: spend the most time here. Investors: show the alert and the ₹ figure; skip capture details.
**5.** "Five layers: read the scale, estimate visible quality, attribute a cause from waiting and heat, alert, summarise. Every figure shows its rule. A scheduled job checks lots every 15 minutes and alerts without anyone asking." Jury: explainability and depth. Investors: prevention is the value.
**6.** "Phone only, offline, Telugu, free tiers for the pilot. In-app camera, server time and a GPS check make cheating visible. Limits: AI is an estimate, staff confirm every reading, thresholds need calibration." Jury: feasibility and honesty. Investors: low cost to serve.
**7.** "Buyers are processors, exporters, aggregators, large FPOs. Prices are proposed, not validated. Illustrative: on ₹10 crore procured, one percent less loss is ₹10 lakh." Read the market formula only with sourced inputs. Jury: move quickly. Investors: slow down; the pilot is how prices get validated.
**8.** "Traceability and grading tools answer other questions; we answer where, when and why value was lost. Based on public positioning, not a feature audit." Investors: be ready for "what stops them adding it?".
**9.** "Four people benefit. Start with one to three pilots, then clusters, then crops and states. Calibration and farmer payment reports are next, not built." Investors: the pilot is the next milestone.
**10.** State the ask plainly: pilot partners, mentors, [funding]. "Thank you."
