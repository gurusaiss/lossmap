# Q&A backup (fuller versions in docs/JURY_QA.md)

**Solutions already exist. What's unique?** (slides 5, 8) Traceability records where produce came from; grading scores quality at one point. We compare consecutive weighing points to show where value was lost, in rupees, attribute a probable cause from waiting and heat, and alert before spoilage. We describe others from public positioning and have not audited their features.

**Who will buy? Business model?** (slide 7) Processors, exporters, aggregators and large FPOs who pay for produce that arrives spoiled. Proposed: free 30-day pilot, then per-centre monthly plans. Not validated; the pilot is how we validate.

**There's already a person at the checkpoint. Why software?** (slides 3, 6) They weigh, but the number is not connected to the lot's history. One scan and two photos put every weight on one timeline. We have not timed this with real staff.

**What if staff or drivers cheat, or a phone is off?** (slide 6) In-app camera only (others flagged), server timestamps, GPS-versus-centre check, immutable photo evidence, audit trail on edits, fixed checkpoints rather than the driver's phone. A switched-off phone saves locally and uploads later. A colluding insider can still photograph a false number; we make it visible, not impossible.

**How accurate is the AI?** (slides 5, 6) Not measured; no claim. The worker confirms every reading; the AI value is stored beside the confirmed one so accuracy can be measured. Quality is a visible estimate, not a grade. If AI fails, typing is instant.

**Does it work offline?** (slides 4, 6) Capture does: saved on the phone and uploaded later, including opening the app with no connection. AI reading, weather and the dashboard need a connection.

**How does it help farmers?** (slide 9) Each lot has a public report with farm-gate weight, quality estimate and photo: evidence at handover. It also shows which step lost the value.

**How will it scale, and what does it cost to run?** (slides 6, 9) ₹0 for the pilot on free tiers. Beyond that: paid database and AI tiers and a commercial weather plan, not yet costed per centre. New crops are settings, not code.

**Have you talked to real users?** (slides 7, 9) **[Fill in honestly.]** We have no customer interviews to cite and the deck makes no traction claim. The next step is the pilot.

**Is the demo data real?** (slide 4) No. It is generated and labelled simulated. Do not present its figures as customer results.
